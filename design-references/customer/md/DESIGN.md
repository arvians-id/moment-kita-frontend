## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the tension between traditional high-craft stationery and fluid digital interaction. Conceived as an haute couture maison for modern celebrations, it bridges tangible bespoke print (heavy cotton stock, blind deboss, foil stamping) with architectural digital experiences (interactive guest suites, digital wax seals, bespoke URL narratives).

The visual language draws heavily from contemporary luxury fashion editorial and tactile minimalism. It balances generous negative space with sharp, deliberate typographic hierarchy. The aesthetic delivers "quiet luxury"—understated confidence, restrained color gestures, hyper-refined micro-borders, and asymmetric editorial layouts that evoke the physical heft of an archival monograph.

Interactions are deliberate and weighted: transitions mimic the sliding of heavy textured cardstock from a lined envelope, and interactive RSVP flows retain the reverence of physical mail rather than a transactional SaaS form.

## Colors
The palette balances luminous mineral foundations with grounded ink tones and earthy warmth:

- **Primary (`#1A1A1A` - Deep Charcoal / Soft Black):** The primary typographic and framing ink. Never harsh `#000000`; it preserves the softness of vintage letterpress ink pressed into unbleached rag paper.
- **Secondary (`#C47B62` - Muted Terracotta):** Used for focal highlights, primary interactive accents, select rule dividers, and curated badges. Evokes aged Tuscan brick, wax seals, and warm sunlit clay.
- **Tertiary (`#9E5241` - Dusty Rosewood):** A deeper, richer crimson-terracotta used for active states, hover transitions, high-contrast badges, and editorial emphasis.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Alabaster / Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas ground across light mode, providing a warm, tactile foundation far softer than sterile white.

### Extended Environmental Tones
- **Champagne / Oatmeal (`#EFE9DE`):** Secondary container surface, layered card backgrounds, and digital envelope sleeves.
- **Muted Sage (`#7D8B7E`):** A rare organic accent reserved for botanical suites, confirmation checkmarks, and subtle RSVP status flags.
- **Onyx Espresso (`#121110`):** Reserved for deliberate editorial inversions—full-bleed dark sections showcasing high-craft printing, foiled textures, or candlelit evening stationery. Accompanied by **Champagne Gold (`#E3D4BE`)** for type and borders within dark environments.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm mirrors traditional editorial typesetting found in luxury fashion hardcovers and boutique invitations:

- **Playfair Display:** Chosen for its razor-sharp transitional serifs, high stroke contrast, and poetic elegance. Display styles should favor italicized emphasis on key emotive words (e.g., *“The Suite”*, *“Tactile Paper”*).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Selected for its subtle calligraphic geometric warmth, offering supreme clarity at small sizes without sterile corporate coldness.
- **Editorial Numbered Kickers:** Section intros, dual product toggles, and metadata tags always utilize `kicker` typography set in full uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.2em`), prepended with typographic em-dashes and numeral prefixes (e.g., `01 — DIGITAL INVITATION`, `02 — ATELIER PRINT`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an asymmetric 12-column architectural grid with generous breathing space. Rather than uniform stacked blocks, content shifts along an editorial rhythm with intentional whitespace and overlapping focal planes.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `3.5rem` outer margins and `2rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas bounded at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.

### Vertical Cadence & Asymmetry
- Inter-section spacing is generous (80px–140px on desktop) to evoke the uncluttered pages of a coffee-table book.
- Hero units and dual-product spotlights feature intentional 1-column offsets (e.g., text spans Columns 2–6, imagery spans Columns 7–12 with an overlapping secondary card bleeding across Columns 6–8).

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with luxury editorial sensibilities, spatial depth avoids synthetic drop shadows. Hierarchy is achieved through **Tonal Layers**, **Ultra-Fine Hairlines**, and **Tactile Ambient Diffusion**:

- **Tonal Stacking:** Surfaces step through warm materiality. Canvas (`#F9F6F0`) hosts Elevated Sleeves (`#EFE9DE`) or pure Alabaster cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards, interactive panels, and modular divisions rely on crisp `1px` borders in low-contrast tones (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)` on light surfaces; `rgba(227, 212, 190, 0.16)` on dark espresso surfaces).
- **Tactile Paper Diffusion:** Floating digital envelopes, suite previews, and modal drawers utilize a diffuse, warm ambient shadow simulating soft natural daylight hitting paper: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(46, 38, 33, 0.07), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(46, 38, 33, 0.03)`.
- **Moody Espresso Inversions:** In inverted editorial chapters (`#121110`), surfaces use semi-transparent inner paneling (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`) with subtle champagne borders (`#E3D4BE` at 20% opacity) to evoke gold foil stamped on dark cardstock.

## Shapes
A level of `0` (Sharp) defines the core architecture. All structural cards, interactive tabs, editorial containers, and primary action buttons adhere to strict `0px` border-radii. This reflects the guillotine-cut edges of hand-torn paper, luxury envelopes, and bespoke letterpress blocks.

**Exceptions:**
- **Pill Badges & Wax Seals:** Circular seals, status pips, and discrete kicker chips use full pill curvature (`9999px`) to contrast against the razor-sharp rectilinear grid.
- **Digital Envelope Liners:** Digital flap components incorporate deliberate geometric angles (45-degree envelope triangles) rather than curved folds.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Atelier Button:** Solid `#1A1A1A` fill, crisp `#FFFFFF` text, `0px` radius, padding `14px 28px`. Typography: `label-caps`. Hover states shift gently to `#C47B62` with a `250ms ease-out` transition.
- **Secondary Linen Button:** Background transparent, `1px` border in `#1A1A1A`, text `#1A1A1A`. Hover fills with `#EFE9DE` or shifts border to `#C47B62`.
- **Editorial Text Action:** Pure typographic link in `label-caps` underlined by a hairline `1px` rule offset by `6px`. Hover moves the line with an animated sliding underline.
- **Concierge Button (WhatsApp):** Sharp `#1A1A1A` button adorned with a fine Champagne Gold (`#E3D4BE`) hairline border and a subtle terracotta monogram pip.

### Dual-Line Product Cards
- **Digital Invitation Suite Card:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card against `#F9F6F0` canvas. Top edge bears a numbered kicker (`01 — DIGITAL EXPERIENCE`). Houses interactive interactive RSVP preview, animated envelope trigger, and live URL badge.
- **Tactile Print Atelier Card:** Textured `#EFE9DE` card with blind deboss border simulation (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`). Spotlights paper weight specifications (e.g., *600 GSM Cotton*), foil finish swatches, and direct WhatsApp concierge consultation triggers.

### Form Inputs & Interactive RSVPs
- **Minimal Text Inputs:** Underline-only or crisp rectangular outline with transparent ground. Hairline border `#1A1A1A` at 20% opacity, transitioning to `#C47B62` on focus with zero outline ring. Typography is set in `body-md`.
- **Radio & Checkbox Selectors:** Square sharp boxes (`0px` radius) with `1px` charcoal borders. Selected state renders a centered terracotta fill pip or delicate crosshair.
- **Guest Counter & Meal Choice:** Minimalist stepped stepper flanked by subtle `+` and `−` glyphs with hairline vertical dividers.

### Badges, Kickers & Chips
- **Numbered Kicker Badge:** Inline uppercase text (`label-caps`) with an em-dash prefix (`01 — ATELIER`). Never encased in heavy bubbles.
- **Wax Seal Pip:** Circular SVG component (`36px × 36px`) in `#C47B62` with a debossed monogram impression, serving as an interactive seal for digital opening animations.

### Media & Overlapping Editorial Panels
- **Asymmetric Image Frames:** Photography of stationery suites, typography specimens, and real weddings are framed in sharp rectangular viewports, frequently paired with overlapping caption cards spanning across grid columns.
