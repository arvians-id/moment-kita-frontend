Punctual Clarifications

Frequently Inquired Matters

Essential details on studio timelines, planner collaboration, and physical consultations.

01 How quickly will the studio respond to my inquiry? expand_more
During standard Paris (CET) and New York (EST) operating hours, our typographers reply to all correspondence within 2 to 4 business hours. For immediate turnaround, our WhatsApp Concierge is continuously manned during European daytime.
02 Can my wedding planner or coordinator liaise with you directly? expand_more
Certainly. Over 60% of our bespoke print commissions and custom digital portals are coordinated alongside certified event planners. We provide dedicated shared moodboards, vectorized proofing sheets, and multi-admin digital RSVP portal access upon invitation.
03 Do you offer physical in-person studio consultations? expand_more
Yes. We host private, complimentary 45-minute studio consultations at our salon spaces in Paris (6th Arrondissement) and New York (DUMBO, Brooklyn) by scheduled appointment. Simply note your city preference in the correspondence form or book directly via our WhatsApp Concierge.
