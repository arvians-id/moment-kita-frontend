devices Section 01

Digital Invitations (SaaS)

9 Curated Inquiries

How do I edit my wedding information and schedule after publishing?

add

All digital invitations run on real-time cloud synchronization. You can log into your dashboard at any point—even minutes before your ceremony—to alter timing, update venue GPS pin coordinates, swap dinner courses, or announce weather-related contingency venues. All published links, custom URLs, and guest QR envelopes reflect edits instantaneously without breaking existing links or requiring re-sending.

Can I switch to a different design template without losing my guest list or data?

add

Yes. Our architectural architecture separates your celebration data layer from typography layouts. You can freely test and transition between aesthetic themes (such as transitioning from Minimalist Vellum to Château Noir) with zero data loss. Your guest attendee matrices, RSVP selections, custom dietary responses, and high-resolution photo galleries migrate flawlessly.

How does connecting a custom domain URL work (e.g., sophia-alex.love)?

add

Included with all Signature and Prestige suites is bespoke domain mapping. You can connect an existing domain purchased through any registrar (e.g. GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare) in two clicks, or let our white-glove team register a clean modern TLD on your behalf. We provision automated, bank-grade SSL certificates worldwide within 15 minutes of DNS verification.

What is the lifetime cloud hosting duration, and are there renewal fees?

add

We do not believe in expiring wedding memories. All L'Atelier Nuance digital suites include guaranteed 5-year active cloud permanence at zero recurring renewal fees. Following your wedding, your invitation converts to a private commemorative monograph archive preserving audio wishes, guestbook signatures, and wedding photo memories intact.

How does the real-time RSVP and dietary matrix tracker work?

add

Guests receive an elegant digital envelope unsealing animation leading directly to your personalized RSVP portal. They can confirm plus-ones, choose multi-course culinary options, specify critical allergen notes, and respond to custom sub-events (e.g. Welcome Dinner, Rehearsal, Morning Recovery Brunch). All inputs populate an exportable spreadsheet and live visual matrix formatted for catering coordinators.

How many photos and 4K videos can we upload to our gallery?

add

Essential tiers accommodate up to 40 high-resolution editorial photos. Signature and Prestige tiers offer uncompressed, unlimited visual archives with direct 4K video reel streaming powered by global edge CDN distribution, ensuring lightning-quick load times on both mobile and desktop screens.

Can we add custom background music or Spotify playlists to our digital invitation?

add

Yes. You may upload an acoustic audio track of your choice (MP3/WAV with licensed rights or royalty-free audio) accompanied by a tasteful, unobtrusive floating audio player with custom volume controls. Alternatively, you can embed an interactive Spotify or Apple Music celebration playlist directly into your weekend itinerary.

How does the digital cash envelope and registry work (do you take a fee)?

add

L'Atelier Nuance takes 0% commission on your gifts. You can configure direct IBAN/SWIFT banking transfers, Zelle, Venmo handles, or direct QR codes. Guests pay directly into your verified bank or honeymoon fund with zero platform deductions or hidden escrow delays.

How do we publish, password-protect, and distribute our wedding invitation link?

add

When ready, click "Publish Suite." You can enable private global password protection or unique passcode access per guest tier. For dissemination, we supply clean social share cards, automated WhatsApp one-click distribution links, and downloadable vector QR codes formatted to print onto physical cards.

print Section 02

Printed Stationery & Fine Craft

6 Curated Inquiries

What is the minimum order quantity for bespoke letterpress suites?

add

Because each stationery commission requires custom CNC magnesium or photopolymer press plate engraving and manual press ink calibration on our vintage Heidelberg presses, our baseline minimum order is 50 finished suites. Additional increments are available in batches of 25.

What is the production timeline from proof approval to arrival?

add

Design collaboration and 1-on-1 typographic proofing typically takes 1 to 2 weeks. Once you provide signed digital proof clearance, artisanal press production takes 3 to 5 weeks. Express studio queue expedited manufacturing (10 business days) is available upon consultation for impending wedding dates.

What paper stocks and tactile finishes do you offer?

add

We source exclusively from historic paper mills in Great Britain and Italy: 600gsm to 900gsm 100% wild cotton rags, translucent frosted vellum, blind letterpress debossing, warm micro-fluted embossing, heated copper-rose and antique champagne gold foil stamping, and organic handmade feathery deckle edges.

Can our wedding monogram or family crest be custom drawn by an atelier artist?

add

Yes. Our Paris studio typographers specialize in bespoke ligature drawing, historical Roman Romanesque monograms, and custom hand-rendered botanicals. Once drafted, your crest is vectorized for both digital invitation headers and carved into custom brass wax seal stamps for physical mail.

How can we touch and feel paper stocks before committing?

add

We encourage ordering our Artisan Sample Box ($25), which showcases our 600gsm cotton cards, foil swatches, hand-poured terracotta wax seals, envelope liners, and paper texture cards. The sample box price is fully credited toward your eventual stationery booking.

What global shipping carriers do you use for delicate stationery delivery?

add

All finished physical stationery suites are sealed in custom archival clam-shell boxes and dispatched via DHL Express with direct signature-on-delivery tracking, ensuring safe transit to any destination worldwide in 2 to 4 business days.

credit_card Section 03

Payments & Investments

5 Curated Inquiries

What payment methods are accepted for digital and printed orders?

add

We process all major international debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, direct ACH wire transfers, and SEPA transfers for European clients. High-value bespoke print suites may also be paid via tailored international invoice.

How quickly is my digital invitation workspace activated upon payment?

add

Immediate. Within seconds of checkout, your digital suite workspace is generated and unlocked in full. You receive an instant email invitation link with secure access keys to start editing typography, dates, and media directly.

Can I upgrade my digital tier (e.g. from Essential to Signature or Prestige) later?

add

Yes. You can upgrade tiers anytime by paying solely the prorated difference between the packages. Your existing customized content and live URL will remain unaffected.

What is the deposit schedule for bespoke printed stationery commissions?

add

Custom physical stationery requires a 50% non-refundable retainer upon design commencement to secure your studio press queue slot and order paper stock. The remaining 50% balance is invoiced only after you review and approve the final digital press proof.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

add

We provide a 14-day money-back guarantee on all digital SaaS tiers if you have not connected an active custom domain. For physical stationery, once press plates are manufactured and paper is trimmed, print fees cannot be refunded due to bespoke tailoring.

Account & Security

4 Curated Inquiries

How do I create an Atelier Nuance account?

add

Accounts are generated automatically upon your initial template selection or order creation. You will receive a secure magic login link via email to access your control center without cumbersome credentials.

Can I sign in using Google or Apple Single Sign-On (SSO)?

add

Yes. We support one-tap biometric authentication via Apple ID and Google Workspace SSO, alongside traditional encrypted email magic links.

How do I reset my password or update my login credentials?

add

Head to the login portal and click "Reset Password." A cryptographically salted reset token will be dispatched to your registered email immediately.

Can my wedding planner or partner access the same invitation dashboard?

add

Absolutely. You can invite your wedding planner, partner, or stationery coordinator as a collaborator under "Team Access" with custom permissions (e.g. Full Edit, RSVP View Only, or Guest List Export).
