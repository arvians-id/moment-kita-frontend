Concierge Advisory

Frequently Answered

Can I transition from a digital invitation to a printed suite later? expand_more
Yes, seamlessly. All typography vectors, monogram crests, and color palettes created in your digital workspace can be imported directly by our print atelier. If you decide to commission 50+ printed suites at any point, your $49 digital fee is credited in full toward your stationery commission.
How do revisions work for printed typography proofs? expand_more
Every printed stationery commission includes three complete rounds of complimentary proofing. We share high-resolution 1:1 scale vector PDFs and render 3D lighting simulations to show exactly how foil stamping and deboss shadows react. You will chat in real-time with your assigned typographer via WhatsApp.
What happens if our wedding date or venue changes after publishing? expand_more
For digital suites, updates reflect instantaneously worldwide on your custom domain without altering guest URLs. If you ordered physical letterpress, we provide emergency reprint options at production cost, or assist you with an elegant digital broadcast announcement card sent to all RSVP recipients.
Can we order a physical sample box before making our decision? expand_more
We highly encourage it. Our curated Swatch Box includes sample sheets of all four cotton paper weights (from 350gsm to 900gsm double-thick), metallic foil stamping chips (Copper, Rose Gold, Champagne, Antique Matte Gold), blind deboss depth swatches, and handmade wax seals. The $25 cost is fully refunded against any subsequent order.
