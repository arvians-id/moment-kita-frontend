03 — Complete Transparency

Frequently Answered Questions

Everything you need to know about our lifetime hosting, print deposits, upgrades, and sample kits.

Are there any recurring monthly subscriptions or hidden renewal costs? expand_more
No. L'Atelier Nuance operates strictly on one-time payments. Once you purchase the Signature or Prestige suite, your cloud invitation remains hosted for a lifetime without any monthly recurring SaaS charges. You will never be asked to pay renewal fees to keep your wedding memories alive.
Can I upgrade from the Essential plan to Signature or Prestige later? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. You can begin drafting your invitation on the Essential tier and upgrade to Signature or Prestige at any time. 100% of your initial $49 investment will be credited directly toward the higher tier in your studio dashboard.
How does the Printed Stationery deposit and production workflow operate? expand_more
Upon approving your custom quotation via WhatsApp or email, we request a 50% deposit to formulate ink, create custom magnesium deboss dies, and order archival cotton stocks. We then provide digital proofs. The remaining 50% balance is settled only after final letterpress printing, white-glove inspection, and before DHL Express dispatch.
What is your cancellation and refund policy for digital and print orders? expand_more
For Digital Invitation Suites, we offer a 14-day full refund guarantee if you haven't yet published to a custom registered top-level domain. For Printed Fine Stationery, because custom metal deboss dies are manufactured individually, deposits are non-refundable once physical pressing dies have entered CNC milling.
Can I order a physical sample swatch kit before committing to print? expand_more
Yes, our $25 Curated Swatch Kit includes samples of our 450gsm, 600gsm, and 900gsm cotton papers, heated copper-rose foil swatches, botanical wax seal colors, and sample envelopes. The full $25 cost is automatically deducted from any future printed order over $300.
