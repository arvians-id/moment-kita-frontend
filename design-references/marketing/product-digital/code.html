Curator Knowledge

Frequently Answered

Can I change our wedding information after publishing?

expand_more
Yes, completely. Your digital invitation is dynamic. You can edit itineraries, hotel codes, transportation schedules, and weather updates at any time. Changes sync instantly across all guest devices with zero re-sending required.

Can I connect our own custom domain name (e.g. sophia-alex.love)?

expand_more
Yes. On our Curated Digital Suite and Atelier Bespoke tiers, custom domains are fully supported. You can register a brand new domain directly through us or map an existing one you already own with 1-click automatic SSL certification.

Can I switch templates after entering my details?

expand_more
Absolutely. All your wedding schedule items, photos, RSVP responses, and registry links are saved separately in your Atelier vault. You can alternate between Minimal, Rustic, or Nocturne styles whenever you like.

How long does our wedding website remain online?

expand_more
The Essential tier provides 1 full year, and the Curated Suite provides 2 full years of continuous cloud hosting. After the wedding, your site converts into a gorgeous, permanent digital keepsake. Lifetime extensions are readily available.

Is there a limit on the number of guest RSVPs?

expand_more
The Essential tier accommodates up to 150 guests. The Curated Digital Suite and Bespoke tiers offer unlimited guest RSVPs, meal allocations, plus-one configurations, and infinite CSV export capabilities.

Can we receive monetary gifts and link multiple bank accounts?

expand_more
Yes. Unlike standard registry platforms, L'Atelier Nuance takes 0% cut. You can provide direct bank routing details (IBAN, Swift, routing numbers), custom QR codes, Apple Pay links, or Venmo handles directly within your digital envelope.
