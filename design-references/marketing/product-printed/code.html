The Tactile Architecture

Sensory Anatomy of Fine Craft

Unlike industrial digital printing, our heirloom creations are sensory artifacts. Every millimeter is calculated for weight, relief depth, and light play.

layers
01 · Substrate

Archival Cotton Paper

600gsm to 900gsm pure cotton handmade paper milled in historic European mills with naturally feathered, hand-torn deckle edges. Acid-free and preserved for centuries.

Density: 600–900 GSM · 100% Cotton Rag
flare
02 · Illumination

Hot Foil Stamping

Custom magnesium dies heated to 130°C to press metallic foils deep into the fibers. Available in antique copper, rose bronze, champagne, and matte satin gold.

Heat: 130°C Thermal Bond · Precision Foil
texture
03 · Relief

Blind Deboss Sculpting

Multi-level sculptured dies that depress your personal crest into heavy cotton without pigment. It relies entirely on natural shadows and touch to reveal its intricacy.

Depth: 0.8mm Shadow Inset · Tactile Relief
precision_manufacturing
04 · Impression

Vintage Letterpress

Hand-fed onto our restored 1950s Heidelberg "Windmill" Platen presses. Each impression leaves a crisp, deep bite into the soft cotton pulp that you can trace with your fingertips.

Press: 1954 Heidelberg Tiegel · Manual Inking

Envelopes & Vellum Liners

Hand-constructed heavyweight Euro-flap envelopes lined with bespoke translucent vellum illustrations, architectural sketches of your venue, or botanical flourishes.

Envelope: 300gsm Cotton · French Vellum

The Atelier Process

Why we consult via WhatsApp rather than an automated shopping cart.

Heirloom letterpress is bespoke craftsmanship. An impersonal web cart cannot understand your venue's lighting, paper weights, or personalized monogram proportions.

Aesthetic Discovery

Select your preferred edition or share your wedding Pinterest moodboard, venue architecture, and floral tones with our curation team.

02

Dedicated WhatsApp Dialogue

Connect directly with your personal master typographer. Exchange ideas fluidly without waiting days for sluggish email chains.

Tactile Swatch Box

Receive our sample presentation box in 2–3 days to touch real cotton weights, examine foil reflections, and test wax seals at home.

Typographic Proofs

Receive vector layout proofs with unlimited typographic refinements on kerning, ligatures, hierarchy, and personalized crest monograms.

Carefully packaged in archival linen presentation boxes and delivered via DHL Express directly to your residence worldwide.

Guidance & Clarifications

Frequently Answered Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for printed invitations?

expand_more
Our standard production run begins at 50 suites to maintain economic feasibility for custom metal die manufacturing and Heidelberg press setup. Smaller boutique runs (30 suites) are easily accommodated under our Intimate Gatherings tier.

How long does the design and production process take?

expand_more
Typographic proofs are delivered within 48 hours via WhatsApp. Following your final sign-off, production, manual edge tearing, foil stamping, and drying require 3 to 4 weeks. Rush 14-day production is available upon atelier capacity verification.

Can we request physical paper and foil samples before ordering?

expand_more
Yes, wholeheartedly. We recommend ordering our curated Artisan Swatch Kit ($25, fully credited toward your eventual invitation commission). It contains all paper densities, foil shades, ribbon swatches, and wax seal examples.

Why do you take orders via WhatsApp rather than an automated checkout?

expand_more
Heirloom letterpress is an haute-couture medium. Dialogue regarding paper absorbency, plate deboss depth, venue color palettes, and wording etiquette cannot be properly solved by an impersonal web dropdown. Our clients enjoy frictionless 1-on-1 WhatsApp consultations with real typographers.

Can we pair our printed stationery with a matching digital invitation?

expand_more
Yes! Our Harmonization Program ensures your digital wedding website mirrors your exact typography, hand-drawn monogram crest, and color tonalities. Printed cards can also feature subtle micro-QR codes stamped in foil on details inserts.

Do you handle guest addressing and calligraphy?

expand_more
We provide both archival digital calligraphic guest address printing that matches your primary suite typeface, as well as true hand-lettered dip-pen pointed nib calligraphy executed by our studio scribes.
